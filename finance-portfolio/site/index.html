DCF

Equity Valuation — Discounted Cash Flow

What is a company worth, and what does its market price already assume?

0.45%implied growth

The DCF values the equity at $32 against a $27.60 price. But the result that decides the recommendation is the reverse DCF: the market is pricing 0.45% annual revenue growth into a business that grew 4.7% last year.

FCFF · bottom-up WACC · two terminal methods · 20,000-trial Monte Carlo · reverse DCF · comps

FSA

Financial Statement Analysis

Three companies, one industry, one cost shock. Which is actually a good business?

−$23mreal deleveraging

One company reported repaying $250m of term debt over two years. Total drawn debt fell $23m — the revolver absorbed the difference, and grew in every year that dividends plus buybacks exceeded free cash flow.

Six-year ratio suite · five-step DuPont · common-size · accrual ratio · Altman Z

Portfolio
RISK

Portfolio Performance & Risk Analytics

A seven-sleeve diversified portfolio beat the 60/40 by 32bp a year. Was it worth it?

25% → 2%capital → risk

A quarter of the capital sits in bonds and produces 2% of the portfolio's risk; 65% in equities produces 90% of it. It is not a balanced portfolio — it is an equity portfolio with a bond sleeve attached.

Drift-and-rebalance backtest · Euler risk decomposition · exact attribution · capture ratios

OPT

Portfolio Optimizer

Does mean-variance optimisation beat equal weight out of sample?

0.00Sharpe, max-Sharpe rule

Equal weight won. The Sharpe-maximising optimiser delivered a Sharpe of zero with 13× the turnover — and the look-ahead portfolio, the one that knew the future, beat 1/N by two basis points. There was barely a prize to win.

Simplex projection · Lipschitz-stepped projected gradient · Ledoit–Wolf shrinkage · risk parity · walk-forward

ETF

ETF Cost & Tracking Study

Three funds, one index, expense ratios of 0.03%, 0.09% and 0.35%.

−$64,88630-year fee gap

What 0.32 percentage points costs on a $100,000 investment: 8.6% of the final balance, due whether the fund performs or not. The study also shows why two of the three funds cannot legitimately be judged against this index at all.

Tracking difference vs tracking error · unexplained residual · lifetime cost · breakeven · overlap

Method

State the question before the answer

Every write-up opens with what it is trying to find out and closes with what it concluded and what would prove it wrong. A model without a falsification test is an opinion with arithmetic attached.

Report the results that do not help

The covariance shrinkage that did nothing. The diversification that did not pay. The peer set that collapses to a single company. A portfolio in which nothing ever fails is a portfolio that is not measuring anything.

Say where the data came from

The sample data in these projects is generated rather than real, it says so on every front page, and each project documents exactly how to point the same pipeline at real filings or real prices. Conclusions about method transfer; conclusions about markets do not.
